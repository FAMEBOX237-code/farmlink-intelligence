{# ── What is a forecast — explainer ── #}
What is a harvest forecast?
Once your sensor node has collected 28 days of readings, FarmLink analyses the soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and light intensity trends to predict your harvest window. Interested buyers in your region are then alerted automatically so they can plan purchases in advance.

How FarmLink generates your forecast
1
Sensor collects data
Your Arduino node reads soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and light intensity every 30 minutes and sends readings to FarmLink.
2
28-day threshold reached
After 28 days of readings, the FarmLink engine has enough data to identify reliable growth trends for your crop.
3
Forecast generated
A harvest window is predicted with a confidence score. You will see it here and on your dashboard automatically.
4
Buyers alerted
Buyers who have registered alerts for your crop and region are notified so they can plan purchases before harvest.
