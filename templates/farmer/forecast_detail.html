{# How forecasts are generated — explainer card #}
How FarmLink generates your forecast
1
28 days of sensor data — your Arduino node collects soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and light intensity every 30 minutes.
2
Trend analysis — the FarmLink engine analyses the sensor trends against known growth curves for your crop type.
3
Forecast generated — a harvest window is computed with a confidence score. Higher confidence = more consistent sensor readings.
4
Buyers alerted — interested buyers in your region are notified automatically so they can plan purchases in advance.
