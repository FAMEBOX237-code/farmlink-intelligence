How it works

From sensor to sale in four steps.

A simple, automated pipeline for farmers and buyers alike.

  1. 1

    Farmer registers & installs sensor

    Farm profile created. IoT node begins reading soil and weather data. No manual monitoring needed after setup.

  2. 2

    System scores quality automatically

    Every 30 minutes, a quality score is computed from live sensor data — soil moisture, temperature, humidity, light, and rain.

  3. 3

    Harvest forecast is generated

    After 28 days of data, the system predicts the harvest window with a confidence percentage and links it to the listing.

  4. 4

    Buyers receive a pre-order alert

    Registered buyers are notified before the produce even hits the market — sorted by their crop, region, and quality preferences.
