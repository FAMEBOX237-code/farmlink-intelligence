Three unsolved problems

What makes farming in Cameroon hard — and how we fix it

No existing platform in Cameroon addresses all three of these problems together. FarmLink Intelligence does.

Problem 1 — Market invisibility

Farmers are invisible to buyers

Smallholder farmers sell cheaply to middlemen because they have no direct channel to urban buyers. The middleman profits. The farmer and buyer both lose. FarmLink gives every farmer a verified, data-backed listing that buyers can trust and find.

Solved by the marketplace
Problem 2 — Unverifiable quality

Quality claims cannot be trusted

Any farmer can type "premium quality" on any platform. There is no hardware evidence behind it. FarmLink attaches real IoT sensor readings — soil moisture, temperature, humidity, light — to every listing. Quality is measured, not typed.

Solved by the IoT quality score
Problem 3 — Zero supply visibility

Buyers cannot plan ahead

Buyers discover produce only after it is already listed. They cannot forecast when their preferred crop will be available, leading to last-minute procurement and wasted logistics. FarmLink predicts harvest windows from sensor trends and notifies pre-registered buyers automatically.

Solved by harvest forecasting
The pipeline

From farm sensor to buyer inbox

Every 30 minutes, the Arduino sensor node on the farm transmits readings. By the time a buyer sees a listing, the quality score has already been computed from real hardware data.

1
Sensor reads the farm
The Arduino Uno reads soil moisture, temperature, humidity, light, and rainfall every 30 minutes. The DS3231 clock timestamps each reading precisely.
2
Quality score is computed
The QualityScoreEngine reads the last 24 hours of sensor data and computes a score from 0 to 100. The score updates on every listing automatically.
3
Harvest is forecast
After 28 days of sensor data, the system analyses temperature and moisture trends to predict the harvest window — before the farmer even posts a listing.
4
Buyer is notified
Registered buyers with matching pre-order alerts receive an automatic notification. They contact the farmer directly — no middleman, no delay.
