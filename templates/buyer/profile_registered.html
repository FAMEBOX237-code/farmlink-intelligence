{# Why explainer — accordion-style expandable #}

FarmLink Intelligence is designed to connect farmers (sellers) with buyers (purchasers). Farmers have public listings, quality scores, trust scores, and farm profiles — all the information buyers need to make purchasing decisions.

Buyers, by contrast, do not sell anything on FarmLink. Their role is to browse, contact farmers, and register pre-order alerts. Exposing their contact details, buying patterns, or alert registrations to other platform users would be a privacy violation with no corresponding benefit.

If you need to contact a buyer you transacted with, that communication happens through the transaction record, not through their public profile.
